1. The Booth
↳

Four shots. One strip. You decide when each one happens — tap the shutter, get a three count, and take the next whenever you’re ready.

2. Choose Your Frame
↳

Pick the overlay printed on your strip.

3. How It Works
↳
  1. 01 Allow camera access when your phone asks.
  2. 02 Tap the white shutter when you’re ready. Three, two, one.
  3. 03 Do that four times — at your own pace. Undo any shot you don’t like.
  4. 04 Your strip develops, then save it to your phone.
4. Your Strip
↳

No strip yet. Take four shots and it lands here.

Press and hold the strip to save it manually.

5. Event Info
↳

Strips are built on your phone and never uploaded anywhere. Nothing is stored once you close this page — save yours before you go.
