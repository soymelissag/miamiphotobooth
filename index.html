1. The Booth
↳

Hi! I made some of my illustrations into a custom photo booth strip. Classic Miami icons like Dandy Bear, Frankies, the Tropical Park, and Allens inspired my first pattern. Tap on the camera when you’re ready for the frame, and you’ll get a custom photobooth strip!

2. Choose Your Frame
↳

Pick the overlay printed on your strip.

3. How It Works
↳
  1. 01 Enable camera access when your phone asks.
  2. 02 Tap the white shutter when you’re ready. There are four frames per still.
  3. 03 Do that four times, at your own pace. Undo any shot you don’t like.
  4. 04 Your strip develops, and then you save it to your phone.
4. Your Strip
↳

No strip yet. Take four shots and it lands here.

Press and hold the strip to save it manually.

Your strip lives only on this phone. Close this page and it’s gone.
